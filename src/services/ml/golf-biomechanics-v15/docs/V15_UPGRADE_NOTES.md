# V15 upgrade notes

This release extends V14 with a reliability-oriented biomechanics engine. It deliberately avoids unsupported claims of exact monocular 3D truth.

## Major areas
- confidence propagation from landmarks to coaching
- camera-frame normalization and plausibility checks
- anatomical segment consistency and constrained pose repair
- temporal event windows and multi-resolution signals
- golf-specific separation, stability, pressure proxies, sequencing and club relationships
- hand/pose fusion for wrist and grip-related signals
- personalized baselines and hierarchical anomalies
- drill-specific cues for trail-foot stability, glove-logo lead-wrist control, band torque, step-change transition, pocket-pull and punch-ground
- counterfactual and evidence-ranked coaching suggestions
- performance budgets for mobile devices
